
# 📊 Sales Data Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Sales Dashboard** built to analyze **sales performance, profit trends, customer purchasing patterns, and regional business performance**. This dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics and make data-driven decisions through dynamic filters and visualizations.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales data across different regions, states, product categories, and shipping modes. It helps identify high-performing products, profitable regions, and sales trends over multiple years.

---

## 📊 Business Insights

- 📈 West Region generated the highest sales revenue.
- 💻 Technology products contributed the largest share of overall sales.
- 📱 Phones and 🪑 Chairs were among the top-selling sub-categories.
- 🖨️ Copiers generated the highest profit.
- 🎯 Interactive filters allow quick comparison across years, regions, and categories.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to provide a centralized reporting solution that enables businesses to:

- ✅ Monitor sales performance
- ✅ Identify profitable products
- ✅ Compare regional performance
- ✅ Analyze customer purchasing trends
- ✅ Support strategic business decisions using interactive visualizations
